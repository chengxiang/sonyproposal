**Independent review: How Representations and Weights Shape Multimodal Generation**

**Reading basis.** I read the complete isolated snapshot at `/tmp/sony_independent_review_current/proposal_draft.md`, inspected its two supplied PNG figures, and checked the supplied PDF's page count. I had no access to prior proposal discussion, earlier versions, previous reviews, decisions, repository history, or memories. I consulted the official Sony call and targeted primary sources, including a separate bounded primary-source check of the closest diffusion papers. This review evaluates the snapshot on its own; it makes no comparison with earlier work on the proposal. No proposal files were edited.

**Overall funding assessment: borderline, leaning positive.** This is a credible and strongly theme-aligned research proposal with a concrete implementation path and unusually explicit safeguards for several difficult issues. Its strongest possible contribution is a validated connection between useful conditional information and computations that can be identified, selectively controlled, and reused. I would consider it fundable exploratory research, but would not yet give it an unqualified recommendation: the present comparisons do not fully distinguish that central contribution from engineered information routing, and the relationship between the caption diagnostic and full-prompt generation needs clarification. The revisions below can be made as changes to the argument and planned protocols, with small-study controls accommodated within the existing program. They do not require new preliminary experiments before submission.

The fit to Sony is substantive. The call explicitly seeks accounts of internal representations, modality fusion, attention pathways, generation dynamics, and causal influence on intermediate states and final outputs. The proposed information-path studies and role/attribute-binding demonstration address several of these priorities directly. The call also highlights knowledge externalization and provenance, but does not require every proposal to cover every listed direction; omitting those subjects is not a defect here. The games, animation, and film use case is sufficiently concrete for an exploratory research award, without requiring a production deployment or video deliverable. [Official Sony Focused Research Award call and submission guidance](https://www.sony.com/en/SonyInfo/research-award-program/#FocusedResearchAward)

The strongest aspects are:

- **The proposal is coherent enough to implement.** It defines five fixed components, the corruption paths, rate targets, monotone schedules, independent-progress training, a conditional-information allocation objective, module-access selection, and progress-dependent gates. Figure 1 makes the relationship among these elements intelligible. These are substantially more concrete than a generic promise to interpret multimodal representations.
- **Several important scientific qualifications are already present.** The dependency measure is explicitly model- and context-specific, not a recovered unique graph. Components need not have human labels. The masked-caption diagnostic removes the word before encoding, keeps full-caption targets at pure noise, and disables the image-latent/backbone route. The creator task is distinguished from that diagnostic. These definitions and controls should be retained.
- **The integration risk is acknowledged in useful technical terms.** The native PixArt corruption and decoding path are retained through an explicit prediction conversion. Oracle semantic states are compared with generated states, with rollout exposure proposed if needed. The official repository does provide 0.6B checkpoints at the proposed 256- and 512-pixel resolutions. [PixArt-Σ official implementation and model list](https://github.com/PixArt-alpha/PixArt-sigma#-available-models)
- **The evaluation and scope have a credible foundation.** The four-way schedule/module comparison, two training seeds, paired prompt-level analysis, blinded raters, intervention/restoration controls, and small-resolution fallback are valuable. Video and identity consistency are extensions, not hidden requirements of the minimum deliverable.
- **The existing evidence supports pursuing the ingredients.** The cited LWD tables support the reported FID figures, including the matched-budget 3.53-to-2.87 comparison. The proposal correctly identifies the 120K-versus-4M comparison as main-training updates and includes the additional 10K phase; it should not be read as a measured end-to-end compute speedup. Figure 2 is presented as motivation for selective reuse, not proof of the proposed semantic mechanism. The language-model comparison acknowledges different training settings. [LWD, Section 4 and Tables 1–2](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.19662v1#S4)

**Ranked material criticisms**

1. **High priority: the central experiment does not yet isolate what the measured dependencies contribute beyond imposed routing and sharing.**

   **Passages:** Section 2.3, “Train restricted attention heads to read only the selected component vectors before unrestricted mixing”; Section 5, “Ordinary versus dependency-guided module access/sharing at matched capacity”; Section 3's comparison table, which summarizes Latent Forcing principally as asynchronous representation generation.

   **Why it matters:** Once a head is assigned a restricted input and target output, finding that it carries that input's effect partly confirms the designed architecture. Likewise, tying a core across tasks creates shared computation by construction. The meaningful research question is whether the measured dependency map predicts a useful choice of routes and sharing, beyond generic sparsity, ordinary multitask tying, or target-specific branches. The existing capacity-matched comparison is useful, but access restriction, choice of access, and sharing remain bundled.

   The prior-work boundary is also narrower than the table suggests. Latent Forcing already measures how predictive usefulness changes across both modalities' noise levels and includes modality-specific output experts. Local Mechanisms already includes feature-space diagnostics and a causal architectural intervention, while Vision-Language Binding directly intervenes on information-routing paths. None of this establishes the proposal's intended component-to-reusable-computation rule, but measuring usefulness, specializing branches, and intervening on paths are not individually sufficient novelty claims. [Latent Forcing, Sections 4.2 and 4.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.11401v1), [Local Mechanisms, Sections 3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.16447v3), [Vision-Language Binding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24624)

   **Proportionate revision:** State one falsifiable prediction linking a dependency measured in an unrestricted checkpoint to a later module effect or transfer benefit. Define “ordinary” explicitly. Within the small-model studies, include an access-pattern control with the same number of permitted inputs and the same sharing architecture, but randomly selected or permuted dependencies. Measure at least one predicted component–module relationship before forcing that route architecturally, or clearly distinguish discovery in the unrestricted model from verification of the constructed model. Expand the closest-work table accordingly. This need not add another full PixArt factorial study.

2. **High priority: the full-prompt task appears to generate language states that are already deterministically available from its input.**

   **Passages:** Section 2.1, “Full-caption states and image features supply training targets; the generator must produce these states at inference”; Section 2.2, “conditioning c, which contains the supplied prompt or incomplete caption”; Section 5, “A creator enters a complete description.”

   **Why it matters:** As written, the complete caption used to construct Qwen targets appears to be the same complete description supplied to the creator system. If so, those targets can be obtained by running the frozen encoder on known text. Diffusing them is an artificial prediction problem, not a necessity for acquiring unknown information. That can still be a useful auxiliary training or computation mechanism, but its role needs a different justification. It also makes it unclear whether a learned language schedule reveals a useful generation dependency or mainly allocates effort to reconstructing an already supplied condition. The masked-caption diagnostic does not have this problem: its missing content is genuinely unavailable.

   **Proportionate revision:** Specify, separately for each task, which states are known conditions and which contain unknown generation targets. For full-prompt generation, either keep the known Qwen prompt states clean, or explicitly explain what additional scene information the generated language targets contain beyond the prompt. If the intent is to denoise the known caption as an auxiliary mechanism, include a small-stage comparison with clean, directly encoded prompt states and identify the benefit that would justify this choice. This is a clarification and baseline issue, not a claim that joint language/image diffusion is invalid.

3. **High priority: the preferred role-binding endpoint needs an explicit visual-observability and ambiguity rule.**

   **Passages:** Section 6, “Handover is the preferred directional interaction, subject to annotation coverage”; Section 5, “The main endpoint is the fraction of images satisfying all specified participant, attribute, and role requirements”; Section 2.2, “matched images with different giver–receiver assignments.”

   **Why it matters:** Some single images of a shared object and two reaching people are compatible with opposite transfer directions. Annotation coverage and a role-aware evaluator do not by themselves establish that the intended giver is visually identifiable. If labels rely on inferred temporal intent, the compatibility model, conditional diagnostic, and final metric can reward the same convention or shortcut rather than grounded role binding. This is a task-design risk, not a claim that handover can never be evaluated from a still image. The proposal already supplies human checks and held-out compositions; the missing point is what visual evidence makes a directional label decidable.

   **Proportionate revision:** Add a short rubric specifying the visible cues required for the chosen relation, and make role observability part of the month-3 interaction-family decision. Include an “ambiguous/not visually decidable” judgment and specify its treatment in the primary denominator; ambiguity should not silently count as success or be removed after seeing method results. Ensure the checked mechanistic cases contain identifiable directional evidence. If coverage is inadequate, choose one relation with clearer static asymmetry rather than enlarging the task. No new pre-submission annotation audit is necessary.

4. **Medium priority: component swaps need a semantic nuisance control in addition to module-damage controls.**

   **Passages:** Section 2.1 defines each visual component as a coordinate group collected across all image tokens; Section 2.3 proposes to “Exchange that component between the two images,” followed by module removal/restoration, random-module interventions, and perturbation-magnitude controls.

   **Why it matters:** A global PCA coordinate group can carry pose, clothing, background, and other image differences together. Swapping only one group can also combine mutually inconsistent features from two images. The proposed module controls address general damage, and matched role swaps are a good start, but neither alone establishes that the component's mediated effect is specifically about role information. “Matched” currently does not state which visual nuisance factors must be matched. This concern remains even though the proposal correctly avoids claiming a unique causal graph. Activation-patching methodology itself stresses that conclusions concern the differences actually varied by the source and destination cases. [How to Use and Interpret Activation Patching, Sections 2.6 and 3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.15255v1)

   **Proportionate revision:** Define the matching criteria and include a within-role swap control that changes comparable nuisance content while preserving the role label. State the expected direction of the role-prediction change, not just a change in reconstruction error. Keep the existing restoration, random-module, and collateral-effect controls. Where partially swapped states visibly violate feature consistency, report the result as sensitivity to that intervention unless a targeted matched control supports the stronger role-specific interpretation. These controls can be drawn from the already planned 100–300 cases.

5. **Medium priority: the compute requirement is estimated but its source is not established.**

   **Passages:** Section 6, “approximately 5,000 H100-80GB GPU-hours,” including eight four-GPU main runs; Budget basis, “No funding is requested for equipment, supplies, travel, other expenses, or subcontracts.”

   **Why it matters:** The runtime table and month-3 profiling are sensible, and I have no basis to call the assumed throughput impossible. However, the snapshot does not identify an existing allocation, institutional resource, or other support providing the required GPUs. A detailed resource estimate is not the same as access to the resource. With the entire request committed to personnel, tuition, and indirect costs, the execution plan depends on an unstated contribution. This is more consequential than further refining seconds per update.

   **Proportionate revision:** Add one factual sentence naming the available compute source, approximate allocation, and ability to run four GPUs concurrently. If access is only anticipated, state the contingency and the smaller program that it would support. Do not imply a confirmed allocation without one. The current request is below Sony's $150,000 cap, but the call makes the award inclusive of associated expenses; the treatment of compute should therefore be explicit. [Sony budget guidance](https://www.sony.com/en/SonyInfo/research-award-program/#FocusedResearchAward)

6. **Medium priority: the visual-quality constraint is part of success, but has no operational measure.**

   **Passages:** Section 5 repeatedly promises to measure “image quality” and requires improvement “without a material loss of visual quality.”

   **Why it matters:** The semantic endpoint is comparatively concrete, whereas this second success condition is not. A method could improve detectable attributes or roles by producing rigid, simplified scenes while reducing asset usefulness. Nothing in the proposed prompt-level bootstrap or automated role validation resolves how that tradeoff will be judged. The existing two-rater budget could support a quality assessment, but the text currently assigns those raters to validating role correctness rather than specifying a quality outcome.

   **Proportionate revision:** Name one primary quality measure appropriate to the small focused test set, such as blinded paired judgments of visual plausibility and artifacts, and define a tolerable degradation rule before final held-out evaluation. Reuse the planned common human subset where practical. Specify whether the measure compares each main variant with the ordinary-module baseline, unmodified PixArt, or both. This is a short protocol addition, not a request for another benchmark suite.

**Limits of this assessment.** I checked the public papers' reported results, not their underlying experiments. I did not independently verify the unpublished Adaptive Patchwise Denoisers, weight-restoration, or language-model measurements; the snapshot labels them preliminary and they are reasonable evidence of ingredients rather than demonstrations of the proposed connection. I did not audit data coverage, actual cluster availability, runtime, or all implementation details. The schedule objective and native-noising conversion provide a concrete research starting point; I found no basis in this reading to declare them mathematically invalid. Conversely, their notation does not establish that measured dependencies will predict transferable mechanisms—that is the central question the experimental design must discriminate.

The supplied PDF has 10 pages and is approximately 257 KB, within the call's overall 11-page and 16 MB limits. The stated request is within the award cap. I did not perform a full typography or submission-package compliance audit. Scientifically, the proposal's greatest asset is that it offers a bounded way to connect an internal causal account with useful generation; its priority revision is to make the distinctive connection testable independently of the structure deliberately built into the model.
